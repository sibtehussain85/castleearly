New to chess? Start with these five rules

  1. Fight for the center. Pawns on e4, d4, e5 or d5 give your pieces room.
  2. Develop your knights and bishops in the first few moves.
  3. Castle early to keep your king safe. It's so important we named the site after it.
  4. Don't bring your queen out too early, or she'll get chased around.
  5. Before every move, check what your opponent is attacking.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. You can play as many games as you like without an account. The site is supported by ads.

How strong is the computer?

Easy makes mistakes and is good for beginners. Medium plays sensible chess. Hard looks several moves ahead and punishes blunders. Master is Stockfish 19 lite, a world-class engine that's stronger than any human player.

Can I play against a friend?

Yes. Choose "vs Friend" and take turns on the same phone, tablet or computer.

Is there a free game analysis?

Yes. After any game, press Analyze game. Every move gets a label (best, strong, inaccuracy, mistake, blunder and more), you see your accuracy, an advantage graph, and the better move whenever you went wrong. The analysis is done by Stockfish 19 lite, running in your browser, so it's free and unlimited.

Will my game be saved?

Your current game is saved in your own browser, so you can come back and continue. Nothing is sent to us.
