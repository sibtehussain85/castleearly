About CastleEarly

CastleEarly is a free website that teaches chess openings to beginners and improving players. Each lesson comes with an interactive board, so you can play through the moves instead of just reading them, and a Practice mode that checks whether you remember the line.

Why "CastleEarly"?

"Castle early" is one of the first pieces of advice every chess player hears. It sums up what this site is about: simple, reliable principles that help you get a good position out of the opening, without memorizing endless theory.

What CastleEarly is for

CastleEarly is here to help you learn chess and get better at it, one step at a time. You can play the computer, learn openings on interactive boards, solve puzzles and analyze your own games, all for free and without an account.

We want to keep making CastleEarly better, and your opinion helps the most. Please leave a review or send us feedback: tell us what you like, what's missing, or which opening you'd like to learn next.

How lessons are written

Every line on the site is checked move by move. Explanations focus on why a move is played, not just which move comes next. If you spot a mistake, please let me know and I'll fix it.

Is CastleEarly free?

Yes. The site is free to use and is supported by advertising. See the privacy policy for details on how ads and cookies work here.
