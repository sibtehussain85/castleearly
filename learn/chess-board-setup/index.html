How to set up a chess board

By CastleEarlyUpdated November 17, 20265 min read

Setting up the board takes less than a minute once you know the pattern. There are only two things beginners usually get wrong: which way the board faces, and where the king and queen go. Here's the whole setup in five steps.

Setting up a chess board takes less than a minute once you know three things: which way the board goes, where the pieces stand in the back row, and which square the queen starts on. Get those right and every game, lesson and puzzle will look the way it should.

Quick answer

Turn the board so each player has a light square in the bottom-right corner. Put the rooks in the corners, the knights next to them, then the bishops. The queen goes on her own color (white queen on d1, black queen on d8), the king next to her, and the pawns fill the row in front.

The starting position

This is what the board looks like when everything is set up correctly. Notice the light square in the bottom-right corner, and the white queen on a light square (d1).

You can tap the white pieces to see their first moves.

Step by step

  1. Turn the board the right way. Each player should have a light square in the bottom-right corner. Remember: "light on the right."
  2. Rooks in the corners. Put the rooks on a1 and h1 for White, a8 and h8 for Black.
  3. Knights next to the rooks. b1 and g1 for White, b8 and g8 for Black.
  4. Bishops next to the knights. c1 and f1 for White, c8 and f8 for Black.
  5. Queen on her own color. The white queen goes on the light square d1; the black queen goes on the dark square d8. The kings take the last squares, e1 and e8.

Finally, fill the second rank with White's pawns and the seventh rank with Black's pawns. The kings and queens face each other across the board.

Easy memory tricks

  • "Light on the right": the corner square on your right is always light.
  • "Queen on her color": white queen on a light square, black queen on a dark square.
  • Mirror image: Black's pieces are directly across from White's, piece for piece.

That makes 16 pieces for each player and 32 in total, on a board of 64 squares: eight rows and eight columns. The square names come from chess notation, where the columns are lettered a to h and the rows numbered 1 to 8 from White's side.

Which way does the board go?

A chess board has to be turned so that the square in each player's bottom-right corner is light. Seen from White's side, a1 in the bottom-left is dark and h1 in the bottom-right is light.

Many boards print letters and numbers around the edge. If yours does, the letters a to h should run left to right along White's side, and the number 1 should be next to White's first row. If the letters read backwards from where you sit, you are on Black's side.

Who moves first?

White always moves first. In a casual game, a common way to choose colors is for one player to hide a white pawn in one hand and a black pawn in the other, and the opponent picks a hand. After that, players usually swap colors each game. Online, the colors are assigned for you.

White's most popular first moves are 1.e4 and 1.d4. See the best openings for beginners for what to play next.

Common setup mistakes

  • The board is turned sideways, with a dark square in the bottom-right corner.
  • King and queen swapped. If the white queen is on a dark square, swap them.
  • Knights and bishops swapped. Knights always stand next to the rooks.
  • The kings are not facing each other. Both kings go on the e-file and both queens on the d-file, directly opposite each other.
  • Black moves first. White always starts the game.

Frequently asked questions

Which side of the board does White start on?

It doesn't matter which side of the table, as long as each player has a light square in their bottom-right corner. White's pieces start on ranks 1 and 2.

Does the queen always go on her own color?

Yes. The white queen starts on a light square (d1), the black queen on a dark square (d8).

Where does the king go?

Next to the queen, on the e-file: e1 for White and e8 for Black.

How many pieces does each player have in chess?

Sixteen: eight pawns, two rooks, two knights, two bishops, one queen and one king. That is 32 pieces in total.

How many squares are on a chess board?

64, arranged in eight rows (ranks) and eight columns (files), alternating light and dark.

Which color square goes in the bottom-right corner?

A light square. Remember "light on the right": each player should have a light square in their bottom-right corner.

Does the king go on the left or the right of the queen?

From White's side, the king is to the right of the queen: queen on d1, king on e1. Black's king and queen sit directly opposite, on e8 and d8.

Who moves first in chess, White or Black?

White always moves first.

The starting position in FEN

Computers and chess sites describe positions with a line of text called FEN (Forsyth–Edwards Notation). The starting position is:

rnbqkbnr/pppppppp/8/8/8/8/PPPPPPPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 0 1

Each group between the slashes is one row, starting from Black's back row. Lowercase letters are black pieces, capitals are white pieces and numbers count empty squares. The w means White is to move.
