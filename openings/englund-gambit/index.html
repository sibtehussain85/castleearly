Englund Gambit: the trap and how to beat it

By HussainUpdated October 30, 20267 min read

The Englund Gambit is a cheeky reply to 1.d4: Black plays 1...e5 and gives a pawn away straight away. It is not objectively sound, but it hides one of the best-known traps in chess, and plenty of club players have been checkmated by it in under ten moves.

Is the Englund Gambit good?

Not at a high level. Stockfish gives White a clear edge after almost every sensible reply, and strong players know the trap. But below about 1200, many opponents have never seen it, the trap is easy to fall into, and the positions are sharp enough that one slip decides the game. Use it as a surprise weapon in fast games, not as your main defense to 1.d4.

For a reliable answer to 1.d4, learn the Queen's Gambit Declined or the Slav Defense.

Common mistakes

  • As Black, expecting the trap every game. If White plays 4.Nc3 or 4.Qd5, you're simply a pawn down. Know the normal plans too.
  • As White, holding on to the e5 pawn at all costs. Defending it with Bf4 and then Bc3 is exactly how the trap happens.
  • As White, forgetting about c1. Once the bishop has left c1 and the queen has moved, ...Qc1 can be checkmate.

💡 Club-level tip

If you face the Englund as White, don't try to keep the e5 pawn forever. Play Nc3, then e4, and enjoy the center. If you've already gone 4.Bf4 Qb4+ 5.Bd2 Qxb2, the safe move is 6.Nc3.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Englund Gambit sound?

No. With accurate play White keeps an advantage, usually an extra pawn or a big lead in development. It is a trappy surprise weapon, not a main line.

What is the Englund Gambit trap?

1.d4 e5 2.dxe5 Nc6 3.Nf3 Qe7 4.Bf4 Qb4+ 5.Bd2 Qxb2 6.Bc3?? Bb4! 7.Qd2 Bxc3 8.Qxc3 Qc1#.

How do you beat the Englund Gambit?

Take the pawn with 2.dxe5, defend it with 3.Nf3, then develop with 4.Nc3 and 5.e4. If you have played 4.Bf4 Qb4+ 5.Bd2 Qxb2, answer with 6.Nc3, not 6.Bc3.

Who is the Englund Gambit named after?

Fritz Englund, a Swedish player from the early 1900s.

Is the Englund Gambit good for beginners?

As a surprise in blitz it can be fun, and it teaches tactics. As your main defense to 1.d4, choose something solid like the Queen's Gambit Declined.
