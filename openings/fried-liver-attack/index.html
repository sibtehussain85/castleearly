Fried Liver Attack: a complete beginner's guide

By HussainUpdated October 29, 20268 min read

The Fried Liver Attack is one of the most famous attacking ideas in chess. White attacks Black's weakest square, f7, with a knight and a bishop, and if Black defends the wrong way, White sacrifices the knight on f7 to drag Black's king into the middle of the board. It comes from the Two Knights Defense of the Italian Game, and it has been played for more than 400 years. Every 1.e4 e5 player meets it sooner or later, so it's worth knowing from both sides.

Quick answer

The Fried Liver Attack is White sacrifices a knight on f7 to drag Black's king into the center and attack it. Black avoids it by playing instead of , which gives an equal game.

How the Fried Liver works

At the start of the game the f7 pawn is only defended by Black's king. The bishop on c4 and the knight on g5 both attack it, so Black has to react at once. After , Black has to choose how to win the pawn back, and that choice decides everything.

❓ Quick check

After 4.Ng5, which two White pieces attack f7?

Bc4 and Ng5 both aim at f7, and only Black's king defends it.

White's plan

  • Attack f7 with the bishop on c4 and the knight on g5.
  • If Black recaptures with , sacrifice with (or play the even stronger , the Lolli Attack).
  • Keep the king in the center: Qf3+, Nc3, d4 and bring every piece into the attack before Black can escape.

Black's plan

  • Don't play . Play , attacking the bishop on c4.
  • Give back the pawn to develop fast: ...c6, ...h6, ...e4 and ...Bd6, and use the extra time to attack White's knight.
  • If you love chaos, the Traxler () counterattacks instead, but it's risky (see the FAQ).

❓ Quick check

After 4...d5 5.exd5, what's Black's best reply?

5...Nxd5? allows 6.Nxf7!, and 5...Qxd5? 6.Nc3 wins time on the queen. 5...Na5! kicks the bishop.

How Black should defend: 5...Na5!

This is the line strong players use as Black. Black moves the knight out of the pawn's attack, kicks White's bishop, and gives up a pawn to get fast development. Practice it as Black so you're never caught by the Fried Liver.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Fried Liver Attack good?

Against it's very dangerous, and the engine rates White better after . But Black can avoid it completely with , which leads to an equal game. So it's a great weapon in fast games and against beginners, but not a guaranteed win.

Why is it called the Fried Liver?

The name comes from the Italian "Fegatello", a dish of liver. The idea is that Black's king gets "cooked" in the middle of the board. The line was analyzed by Italian players as early as the 1600s.

What is the Traxler Counterattack?

It's instead of . Black ignores the threat to f7 and attacks White's f2. It's wild and fun, but White's best answer, 5.Bxf7+!, gives White an advantage, so it's mostly used as a surprise in fast games.
