Chess openings for White and Black

Every opening here has an interactive board. Step through the main line, read why each move is played, then switch to Practice mode and play it yourself. There are 18 openings covering the most popular choices for both colors, plus a section on opening traps like Scholar's Mate and the Stafford Gambit.

Quick answer

The best chess openings for beginners are the Italian Game and the London System with White, the Caro-Kann or Scandinavian against 1.e4, and the Queen's Gambit Declined against 1.d4. They all follow the three opening rules: control the center, develop your knights and bishops, and castle early.

Openings for White: 1.e4

Open, active games. Start with the Italian Game or the Four Knights.

1.e4

Italian Game

1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4. Fast development and pressure on f7.
1.e4

Ruy Lopez

1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5. Slow, rich pressure on Black's center.
1.e4

Scotch Game

1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4. Opens the center right away.
1.e4

Vienna Game

1.e4 e5 2.Nc3. A surprise weapon with a quick f4 attack.
1.e4

Four Knights Game

1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Nc3 Nf6. Natural, principled development.
1.e4

Fried Liver Attack

1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4 Nf6 4.Ng5. A wild knight sacrifice on f7.

Openings for White: 1.d4 and 1.c4

Solid, strategic games. The London System is the easiest to learn.

1.d4

London System

1.d4 and 2.Bf4. One solid setup against almost anything.
1.d4

Queen's Gambit

1.d4 d5 2.c4. Pressure on Black's center from move two.
1.c4

English Opening

1.c4. Flexible control of d5 from the side.

Defenses for Black against 1.e4

The Caro-Kann and Scandinavian are the simplest; the Sicilian is the most ambitious.

vs 1.e4

Sicilian Defense

1.e4 c5. The most popular fighting reply to 1.e4.
vs 1.e4

French Defense

1.e4 e6 2.d4 d5. A solid pawn chain and counterattack.
vs 1.e4

Caro-Kann Defense

1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5. Rock-solid, with an active bishop.
vs 1.e4

Scandinavian Defense

1.e4 d5. Simple, direct and easy to learn.
vs 1.e4

Petrov Defense

1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nf6. Solid counterattack on e4.

Defenses for Black against 1.d4

The Queen's Gambit Declined and Slav are solid; the King's Indian is for fighters.

vs 1.d4

Queen's Gambit Declined

1.d4 d5 2.c4 e6. Classical, solid and trusted.
vs 1.d4

Slav Defense

1.d4 d5 2.c4 c6. Solid support for d5, active bishop.
vs 1.d4

King's Indian Defense

1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 g6. Let White take the center, then attack it.
vs 1.d4

Nimzo-Indian Defense

1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 e6 3.Nc3 Bb4. Pin the knight, fight for e4.
