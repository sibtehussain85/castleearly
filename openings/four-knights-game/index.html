Four Knights Game: a complete beginner's guide

By HussainUpdated October 29, 20266 min read

The Four Knights Game is what you get when both sides bring out their knights first. It's one of the most natural openings in chess: every move follows the basic rules, and the positions are easy to understand. That makes it a great first opening, and strong players still use it when they want a solid game.

Quick answer

The Four Knights Game starts 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Nc3 Nf6, with all four knights developed. It's a solid, symmetrical opening; White's main choices are 4.Bb5 (the Spanish Four Knights) and 4.d4 (the Scotch Four Knights). It's a great way to learn healthy development.

The main line, move by move

The Spanish Four Knights puts the bishop on b5, like the Ruy Lopez. Step through it, then practice White's moves.

Why the Four Knights is a great first opening

You develop pieces toward the center, castle early and never create weaknesses. If you're new to chess, playing the Four Knights teaches the opening principles better than almost any other opening.

❓ Quick check

Which opening rule does the Four Knights follow best?

Knights belong near the center, and here all four get there in three moves.

Plans for both sides

  • Pin the opponent's f-knight with Bg5 (or ...Bg4).
  • Reroute knights with Ne2–g3 to the kingside.
  • Break in the center with d4 (or ...d5) at the right moment.

The Scotch Four Knights

If you prefer open positions, play d4 on move four. The center opens and both sides get active pieces.

Common beginner mistakes in the Four Knights

  • Copying the opponent forever. Symmetry can only last so long; watch for the moment the copy becomes a mistake.
  • Taking on e5 too early. is a known line, but beginners often just lose a piece.
  • Ignoring pins. A pin on the f6/f3 knight can become dangerous if the h-pawn and g-pawn move.

❓ Quick check

After 3...Nf6, why is 4.Nxe5 risky?

The e5 pawn is defended by the c6 knight, so 4.Nxe5 Nxe5 loses a piece unless you know the special 5.d4 line.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Four Knights Game boring?

It's calm, not boring. Because the positions are balanced, the better player usually wins, and you learn a lot about piece play.

What is the Halloween Gambit?

It's a wild line where White sacrifices a knight with to build a big pawn center. It's fun in casual games but risky against careful defenders.
