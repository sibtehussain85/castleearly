King's Gambit: the oldest attacking opening

By CastleEarlyUpdated November 16, 20266 min read

♟ Play this opening vs the Coach Free · every move explained

The King's Gambit is the most romantic opening in chess. On move two White offers the f-pawn to pull Black's e-pawn away from the center and open the f-file for an attack. It was the main weapon of the great attackers of the 1800s, and it still wins fast games at club level today.

The ideas behind the King's Gambit

attacks the pawn on e5. If Black captures, that pawn has left the center, and White can play d4 and control the middle of the board with two pawns. The f-file also opens, so after castling White's rook points straight at f7, the weakest square near Black's king.

White's plan

  • Play d4 and take the whole center.
  • Develop with Nf3 and Bc4, castle, and put a rook on the f-file.
  • Win the f4 pawn back with Bxf4, or keep attacking if Black tries to hold it.

Black's plan

  • Hold the pawn with ...g5, the classical defense, and threaten ...g4.
  • Or give it back with ...d5 for easy development, as in the main line above.
  • Remember ...Qh4+ whenever White forgets to cover h4.

❓ Quick check

Why does White play 3.Nf3 in the King's Gambit Accepted?

With the f-pawn gone, a queen check on h4 is very dangerous. The knight on f3 covers that square.

The King's Gambit Declined

Black doesn't have to take. With Black develops and uses the diagonal that White's f-pawn has just opened. The bishop looks at g1, so White can't castle kingside until it is blocked or traded.

Bishop's Gambit2...exf4 3.Bc4Allows ...Qh4+ on purpose. The king goes to f1 and White gains time on the queen.

❓ Quick check

After 1.e4 e5 2.f4 Bc5, why is 3.fxe5 a mistake?

The f-pawn no longer covers the diagonal, so 3...Qh4+ leads to checkmate or the loss of a rook.

💡 Club-level tip

Below about 1500, most opponents accept the pawn and then don't know what to do. Learn one setup well: Nf3, Bc4, d4, castle, and aim everything at f7. If they play ...g5 and ...g4, your knight goes to e5.

Frequently asked questions

Is the King's Gambit good?

It is playable and dangerous at club level, where its attacking chances are worth more than the pawn. At the very top it is rare, because accurate defense gives Black a comfortable game.

Is the King's Gambit good for beginners?

It teaches development, open lines and attacking play, so it is a fine choice if you enjoy sharp games. You must be ready for your own king to be a little exposed.

Should Black accept the King's Gambit?

Yes, taking with 2...exf4 is the main line and the most testing reply. Declining with 2...Bc5 is a solid alternative if you want a quieter game.

What is the King's Gambit Declined?

Any line where Black doesn't take the f4 pawn. The most common is 2...Bc5, and the sharpest is the Falkbeer Countergambit, 2...d5.

What famous games started with the King's Gambit?

The Immortal Game between Adolf Anderssen and Lionel Kieseritzky in 1851 is the best known. Boris Spassky also used the opening to beat Bobby Fischer in 1960.
