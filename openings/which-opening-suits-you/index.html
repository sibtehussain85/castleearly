Which chess opening suits you?

Answer five quick questions about how you like to play. You'll get the openings that fit your style, for White and for Black, each with a free interactive lesson.

What the quiz asks

There are five questions, and each one takes a few seconds:

  1. Which color you need an opening for: White, Black or both.
  2. What kind of games you enjoy: attacking, solid or slow and strategic.
  3. How much you want to memorize: as little as possible, or as much as it takes.
  4. Which positions feel comfortable: open boards full of tactics, or closed ones where you maneuver.
  5. Your level: new to chess, improving, or experienced.

Nothing is saved and there is no sign-up. You can take it again as often as you like and compare the results.

How the quiz picks your openings

Every opening on CastleEarly is rated for how attacking, solid or strategic it is, how much theory it needs, whether it leads to open or closed positions, and how experienced you should be to enjoy it. Your answers are matched against those ratings.

As Black you need two answers: one against 1.e4 and one against 1.d4, because White decides the first move. As White, one opening is enough to start with.

Tips for choosing

  • Pick one and stick with it for a few months. You learn more from 50 games in one opening than 5 games in ten.
  • Understand the ideas, not just the moves. Every lesson has key ideas, plans and a quiz.
  • Not sure yet? The Italian Game (White) and the Caro-Kann (Black) are safe first choices.

Want the full overview first? Read the best chess openings for beginners.

Which openings can the quiz suggest?

  • For White: the Italian Game, Ruy Lopez, Scotch Game, Vienna Game, Four Knights Game, Fried Liver Attack, London System, Queen's Gambit and English Opening.
  • For Black against 1.e4: the Sicilian, French, Caro-Kann, Scandinavian and Petrov.
  • For Black against 1.d4: the Queen's Gambit Declined, Slav, King's Indian and Nimzo-Indian.

Frequently asked questions

What chess opening should I play?

Play the one that matches how you like to play. Attacking players usually enjoy the Italian Game and the Sicilian, careful players the London System and the Caro-Kann. The quiz above matches your answers to the openings that fit.

How many openings do I need?

Three: one for White, one for Black against 1.e4 and one for Black against 1.d4. That covers almost every game you will play.

Is the opening quiz free?

Yes. It is free, there is no sign-up, and every opening it suggests comes with a free interactive lesson.

What if I don't like the opening I get?

Give it a fair try first, around 20 games. If it still doesn't feel right, take the quiz again and change the answer you were least sure about.
