Clearance: get your own piece out of the way

By CastleEarlyUpdated November 16, 20263 min read

Sometimes the piece that ruins your combination is one of your own. It stands on the square your knight needs, or on the diagonal your queen wants to use. Clearance means moving it away with such force that your opponent has no time to react.

Quick answer

A clearance is a move that vacates a square or opens a line for another of your pieces. The piece that moves away usually does so with check or by sacrificing itself, so the opponent can't use the extra move to defend. When a square is freed it is called square clearance, and when a file, rank or diagonal is opened it is called line clearance.

Also called
Vacating sacrifice, clearance sacrifice
Two types
Square clearance and line clearance
Level
Advanced
Key point
The clearing move must be forcing

What is a clearance sacrifice?

Most tactics are about your opponent's pieces. Clearance is about your own. You see a mate or a fork that would work if only one of your pieces were not in the way, and the question becomes how to remove it without losing time.

A quiet retreat usually isn't enough, because your opponent sees the threat and defends. So the piece leaves with a check or a capture that has to be answered, even if that means giving it up. The move after that is the one you really wanted to play.

How to spot a clearance

  • Look for a strong move that is blocked by your own piece.
  • See whether that piece can move away with check or with a capture that must be answered.
  • Don't worry about its value. If the follow-up is mate, the blocker can be given away for nothing.

How to defend against clearance

  • Look at what your opponent's pieces are blocking. A queen aimed at your king through her own knight is a warning.
  • Guard the square at the end of the line, such as h7, with an extra piece.

Frequently asked questions

What is a clearance sacrifice?

It is a sacrifice that moves one of your own pieces off a square or line so that another of your pieces can use it.

What is the difference between clearance and a discovered attack?

In a discovered attack the piece behind attacks as soon as the front piece moves. In a clearance, the square or line is freed for a move you will play next.

What is the difference between clearance and interference?

Clearance opens a line for your own pieces. Interference closes a line your opponent's pieces need.
