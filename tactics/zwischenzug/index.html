Zwischenzug: the in-between move

By CastleEarlyUpdated November 16, 20263 min read

Your opponent captures something and expects you to capture back. Most of the time you do. But now and then there is a stronger move available first: a check, a mate threat or an attack on a bigger piece. That surprise is the zwischenzug.

Quick answer

A zwischenzug (German for "in-between move") is a move played before the expected one, usually before an obvious recapture. It makes a threat the opponent has to answer straight away, and only then do you play the move they were expecting, now on better terms. It is also called an intermezzo or an intermediate move, and when the in-between move is a check it is sometimes called a zwischenschach.

What is a zwischenzug?

Exchanges feel automatic: I take, you take back. A zwischenzug breaks that rhythm. In the middle of the sequence one player slips in a forcing move that changes the result of the trade.

For an in-between move to work, it has to be more urgent than the recapture your opponent is counting on. Checks are the most reliable, because they can't be ignored. Threats of mate and attacks on the queen come next.

How to spot a zwischenzug

  • Before every recapture, stop for a second. Ask whether you have a check, a capture or a bigger threat first.
  • Check that your piece will still be there to take afterwards. The in-between move only helps if you can complete the trade later.
  • Look for them in your opponent's captures too. When you start an exchange, make sure the other side has no in-between move.

How to avoid being hit by one

  • Don't assume the recapture. When you plan a trade, check every forcing reply your opponent has, not only the obvious one.
  • Look for loose pieces and a weak king in your position before you start exchanging. Those are what an in-between move attacks.

Frequently asked questions

What does zwischenzug mean?

It is German for "in-between move". In chess it means a forcing move played before the expected move, usually before a recapture.

How do you pronounce zwischenzug?

Roughly TSVISH-en-tsook, with the stress on the first syllable.

What is an intermezzo in chess?

Intermezzo is the Italian word for the same idea, and the two terms are used interchangeably: an in-between move.

Is zwischenzug the same as zugzwang?

No. A zwischenzug is an in-between move. Zugzwang is a position where any move you make harms you.
