The discovered attack: move one piece, attack with another

By CastleEarlyUpdated November 16, 20263 min read

In a discovered attack you move one piece and a different piece does the attacking. Because the piece that moves can make a threat of its own at the same time, your opponent faces two problems with only one move to solve them.

Quick answer

A discovered attack happens when you move a piece out of the way and uncover an attack from the piece behind it. If the uncovered attack is a check, it is called a discovered check. The piece that moved is free to capture or threaten something else, which is why discovered attacks so often win material.

What is a discovered attack in chess?

Two of your pieces stand on the same line, with the front piece blocking the back one. When the front piece moves away, the back piece suddenly attacks along the line. That is the discovery.

The power comes from the piece that moves. It gets a free move: it can capture something, attack the queen or give a check of its own, and your opponent still has to deal with the attack that was uncovered. With a discovered check the moving piece can go almost anywhere, even to a square where it could normally be taken, because the king has to be saved first.

How to spot a discovered attack

  • Look for one of your own pieces standing between your bishop, rook or queen and an enemy target.
  • Ask where the front piece can go with a threat. A capture, a check or an attack on the queen is ideal.
  • Look at lines pointing at the enemy king. If only your own piece blocks the line, you have a discovered check ready.

How to defend against discovered attacks

  • Notice the lineup early. When an enemy bishop, rook or queen points at your king or queen through one of its own pieces, move your piece off that line.
  • Keep the pieces on that line defended. A discovered attack on a protected piece is much less dangerous.
  • Capture or block the piece in the back before the front one moves.

Frequently asked questions

What is a discovered attack in chess?

It is an attack that appears when one piece moves out of the way of another. The piece behind attacks along the line that has just been opened.

What is a discovered check?

A discovered attack where the uncovered piece gives check. The piece that moved can do almost anything, because the opponent must answer the check first.

What is the difference between a discovered check and a double check?

In a discovered check only the uncovered piece gives check. In a double check the piece that moves gives check as well, so the king is checked twice and has to move.

Which pieces can give a discovered attack?

The attack itself comes from a bishop, rook or queen, because only they attack along lines. The piece that moves out of the way can be any piece, including a pawn or the king.
