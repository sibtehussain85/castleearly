The pawn breakthrough: sacrifice two to queen one

By CastleEarlyUpdated November 16, 20263 min read

Three pawns face three pawns, and nothing seems to be happening. Then one side gives away two of them, and the third runs through to become a queen. The pawn breakthrough is one of the most surprising ideas in the endgame, and every player should know it by heart.

Quick answer

A pawn breakthrough is a pawn sacrifice, or a series of them, that forces a passed pawn through an enemy pawn chain so it can promote. In the classic position with three pawns each facing one another, the side whose pawns are further advanced pushes the middle pawn first, then sacrifices a second pawn, and the third one queens.

What is a pawn breakthrough in chess?

Take white pawns on a5, b5 and c5 against black pawns on a7, b7 and c7, with both kings too far away to help. White plays b6. Black has to capture, because otherwise the b-pawn takes on a7 or c7 and promotes.

If Black takes with the a-pawn, White pushes c6. The b7 pawn must capture, and now the a-pawn has a clear road: a6, a7, a8. If Black takes with the c-pawn instead, White mirrors the idea with a6, and the c-pawn is the one that gets through.

It works because White's pawns are closer to promotion. Black ends up with extra pawns, but they are much too slow.

When does a breakthrough work?

  • Your pawns are further advanced than your opponent's, ideally on the fifth rank.
  • The enemy king is outside the square of the pawn that will get through, so it can't catch it.
  • Your new queen arrives first, or arrives with check, if your opponent also gets a passed pawn out of the exchange.

How to defend against a breakthrough

  • Keep your king close to the side where the pawns face each other.
  • Don't let enemy pawns reach the fifth rank together. Advance your own pawns to meet them, or trade one off earlier.
  • If it is your move in the classic position, break the pattern first by pushing your own middle pawn one square.

Frequently asked questions

What is a pawn breakthrough?

It is a sacrifice of one or more pawns that clears the way for another pawn to reach the last rank and promote.

Which pawn do you push first in the three against three breakthrough?

The middle one. Whichever way it is captured, you then push the pawn on the far side from the capture, and your third pawn gets through.

Does the breakthrough always win?

Only if the enemy king is too far away to stop the pawn and your opponent's own new passed pawn is too slow. Always count the moves first.
